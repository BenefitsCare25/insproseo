Your enquiry

Send enquiry opens your configured email app with helpdesk@inspro.com.sg, a subject and your enquiry details filled in. Review and send the message in that app. This website does not submit or store the form contents.

Email and telephone

Information you provide directly to Inspro may be used to respond to your enquiry and provide requested services. Contact Inspro with questions about information you have provided.

Sensitive information

Do not include NRIC numbers, medical records, payment details, passwords or complete policy documents in an initial website enquiry. Ask our team for an appropriate channel.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising cookies or store enquiry data in the browser. Interactive selections and checklist progress are temporary. This notice will be updated if optional analytics or other services are added.

External websites

External links, including the MAS directory, are governed by those sites’ own privacy and security practices.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when website services or enquiry-handling processes change.
