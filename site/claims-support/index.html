Common claim questions.

Know where to share documents and who assesses your claim.

Can I upload documents here?

No. Ask our team for the appropriate channel before sharing medical records, identification or complete policy documents.

Can Inspro guarantee my claim?

No. The insurer assesses the claim against the policy terms and supporting evidence.

What if my employee needs a letter of guarantee?

Follow your programme’s hospital and insurer procedures. Contact Inspro for help with the request; issuance is subject to the programme and insurer’s requirements.
