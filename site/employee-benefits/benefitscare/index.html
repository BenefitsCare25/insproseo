What does the listed monthly price include?

The indicative starting prices are S$2 for Starter, S$4 for Flex and S$6 for Premier per employee per month. Your quotation confirms the total premium, taxes, separate fees, benefit limits, eligibility and employee co-payments.

Can a small company request BenefitsCare for SME?

Yes. Share your headcount and workforce profile so Inspro can confirm eligibility, minimum participation and the terms for your chosen plan.

What does panel GP access mean?

A panel is a network of participating clinics. GP means general practitioner. Check the clinic list, treatment benefits, visit limits and any employee co-payment for your plan.

Does every plan include hospital insurance?

Starter focuses on panel GP access. Flex adds non-panel access and flexible benefits. Premier includes inpatient hospitalisation, subject to the insurer’s terms. Confirm limits, eligibility, exclusions and any employee payment before choosing.

What if we have a larger workforce?

Share your headcount, planned growth and benefit priorities with Inspro to review a suitable BenefitsCare plan and service mix.
