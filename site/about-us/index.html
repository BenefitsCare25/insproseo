About Inspro.

A Singapore insurance brokerage serving employers, businesses and individuals.

Incorporated in June 2000, Inspro Insurance Brokers Pte Ltd helps corporate and individual clients structure insurance solutions and review general and group insurance needs.

Our team brings insurance advice, risk management and claims assistance together, working with insurers to arrange comprehensive and competitive cover.

To be one of the leading insurance brokers providing innovative and sustainable solutions with excellent services.
